Reset Protocol The Terrorist game: how to play

One bomb. One puzzle each round. Everyone does it, one by one, while the table watches. One of you is secretly a terrorist who has to slip an extra move into their turn. Find them before they blow the whole thing.

Players4 to 8
Terrorists1 to 3
Each go15 seconds
Puzzles11

01What you need

  • The bomb, switched on (rocker switch). It runs on its own power bank, so no cable is needed.
  • 4 to 8 players, each wearing a headband in their colour. The bomb uses the colours in this order:
1 Red2 Blue3 Green4 Yellow5 White6 Magenta7 Cyan8 Orange

With 5 players, Red to White are in. Stand in a circle in that order if you can.

02The controls

ControlWhat it does
STARTOK, go, and check your answer. Just tap it.
XDelete one key, or start a timed puzzle again. Hold X for 2 seconds to go back to the menu from anywhere.
Keys 1 to 4Number answers, and picking menu lines.
Y B R G buttonsColour answers: yellow, blue, red, green.
Left and right knobsTurn and press. Both also scroll the menus.
MicHears shouts for the shout puzzle.
Motion sensorFeels shakes and tilts. A hard shake explodes the bomb.

03Setting up

  1. Switch on. The main menu appears. Press 3 for Terrorist.
  2. How many players? Turn a knob or use keys 1 and 2, then START.
  3. How many terrorists? 4 players always have 1. With 5 to 7 it's 1 or 2, and with 8 up to 3. START to begin.

04A round

  1. The puzzle. The bomb names this round's puzzle and shows how to do it. Press a knob to watch the demo again, then START.
  2. The secret check. The bomb calls each colour. Take the ball, tap START to see your role where nobody else can, tap START again to hide it, and pass it on.
    Civilians see: "Do it right. Watch for anything extra."
    Terrorists see their secret sabotage, and who the other terrorists are.
  3. Everyone goes. The bomb calls players in a random order. Tap START when you're ready, then you have 15 seconds to do the puzzle while everyone watches.
  4. The vote. Once everyone has gone, the ball goes round again. Each player picks who they think the terrorist is, in private, and locks it with START.
  5. Out. The player with the most votes is out, and the bomb shows whether they were a civilian or a terrorist. Then a new round starts with a new puzzle.

05The puzzles

Everyone does the same puzzle in a round. Each one comes up once before any repeats.

PuzzleWhat you do

Type the mixType a mix of keys and colours, like 3 Y G 1, then START.
Do the sumThe answer is always 1 to 4. Press it, then START.

Stop in the boxA bar sweeps back and forth. Press START while it's inside the box.
Shout on the blocksShout each time the slider is on a block.

Got it wrong?

Nothing is checked until you press START. If it's wrong, everyone hears the same "WRONG", your entry clears and you try again while your clock keeps running. Use X to delete one key, or to start a timed puzzle again. A slip costs you time, never your role.

06Sabotage

Civilians

  • Do the puzzle right.
  • Watch every hand on the ball. You're looking for an extra move, not a mistake.
  • Vote out the terrorist.

Terrorists

  • Do the puzzle right too.
  • Also do your secret move, like PRESS A KEY TWICE, TURN A KNOB or HOLD A COLOUR DOWN, on your own go.
  • When it lands, the bomb buzzes in your hands.
The buzz proves nothing. Half of everyone's goes get a fake buzz, so feeling or seeing a buzz doesn't mean that player is a terrorist. Watch what their hands do instead.

A terrorist who finishes their go without landing their sabotage is caught: they're revealed and out at the end of the round, with no vote.

07Going out

  • BOOM. If your 15 seconds run out, or you shake the bomb too hard on your go, it explodes and you're out on the spot, whoever you are. The round ends there, with no vote.
  • The vote. No skipping, and you can't vote for yourself. Most votes is out. Only one player goes out per round.
  • A tie. One revote between the tied players, and they don't vote. Still tied? The bomb rolls a die between them.
  • The fuse. Each civilian who goes out burns one box of the fuse on screen. It shows how close the terrorists are to winning.

08Winning

Civilians win

When the last terrorist is out.

Terrorists win

When it's 1 terrorist against 1 civilian, or, with 2 or more terrorists, when they outnumber the civilians (like 2 against 1).

09For the host

  • Back to the menu: hold X for 2 seconds.
  • Spectator screen: from the menu, pick Wi-Fi + update with a knob. Join the ResetProtocol network on a phone, tablet or laptop (the password is on the bomb's screen) and open 192.168.4.1 to follow the game live.
  • Handle with care, but don't baby it: normal passing is safe. Only a real shake sets it off.
  • Loud rooms: the shout puzzle learns how noisy the room is, but very loud floors make it harder.
